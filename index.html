Core Principles

The financial guardrails that guide our evaluations

Survival > Brilliance

Avoid wipeouts at all costs. Conservative financial decisions that preserve capital are favored over high-risk, high-reward plays.

Avoid Debt

Debt is viewed as a noose around your neck. We flag any expenses that involve borrowing, financing, or leverage.

Time > Money

After 60, your remaining time becomes the most precious commodity. We evaluate how expenses affect your autonomy and time freedom.

Cut Envy & Drama

Expenses driven by status-seeking, vanity, or keeping up with others are heavily penalized in our evaluations.
